Cast

Ten pedestrians, two states each. walk is the approach, drawn on the lane and swayed by code; down is the "swept off their feet" pose that replaces it on a hit and tumbles to the floor. Each round deals from a shuffled deck so nobody repeats until everyone has walked. Drawn 145 px tall on a 390 × 694 canvas.

Guide

A white line-art character that winks left, then right, on a light frosted scrim over the lanes. It loops under “Wink to sweep them off their feet” until the first wink starts the round. The same line style guides all four games.

Drawn in code

No image files for these. Everything below is canvas, redrawn every frame so it can react to the beat and to the face.

Lanes and targetsTwo lanes at 28% and 72% of the width, judge line at 68% of the height. A pink target ring on each lane with an approach disc that shrinks onto it as the pedestrian arrives, plus a heartbeat ripple after every wink.
HeartsFly from the winking eye (tracked from the face mesh) to the lane, 12 to 26 px, wobbling, in #ff5c8a. A double-eye hit sends a bigger burst.
ResultConfetti in the five palette colours, a big percentage, a one-line verdict, and the people you swept shown one at a time, half in frame, swaying.

Sound

All synthesized with WebAudio except the ending stinger. No copyrighted audio anywhere.

Groove80 bpm, 15-second song. Kick, snare, hats, a bass line in A and a two-bar chord loop (C, Dm, Em, Dm) under a simple melody. Scheduled ahead of time so it never drifts against the chart.
VoicesTen character voices from two band-pass formant filters over a sine or triangle with vibrato. Pitch, slide and wobble differ per voice: young woman 620 Hz, granny 400 Hz with a big wobble, kid highest, big man lowest. A hit makes the character squeak; a miss makes them grumble.
Cues“Go” when the round starts, perfect / good / miss blips, a win chime.
Stingerending-great.mp3 on 50% or better, shared with the salon and jump-rope games.

Rules & input

How a wink becomes a hit.

Round15 seconds at 80 bpm. Pedestrians walk in on the left and right lanes; wink the eye on their side when they reach the ring. Two at once means close both eyes.
Eye trackingMediaPipe Face Landmarker on device, blendshapes eyeBlinkLeft / eyeBlinkRight. An eye counts as closed when its blink value leads the other by more than 0.18, so it adapts to how open your eyes naturally are.
Single vs bothA 70 ms window decides whether one eye or both closed. The judged time is the earlier of "eye started closing" and "closure confirmed", minus 40 ms of camera and model lag.
Timing windowsPerfect ±220 ms, Good ±500 ms. Wider than a tap game on purpose: a wink is slower than a finger.
FallbackIf the tracker only ever sees double blinks, the game switches to both-eyes mode. Practice mode plays with taps and no camera.
Safe zones390 × 694 canvas. No score pills over the face while playing. The judge line keeps a whole character above y = 545 so the hit moment is never covered by the caption area. Add ?zones to the URL to see the overlay.
FlowCamera card → “Looking for your face” → guide on a frosted scrim (“Wink to start”) → the first wink starts the round → 15 s → result. The first pedestrian arrives about 2.7 s after GO so audio and tracking are warm.
